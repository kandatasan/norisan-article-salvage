Getting around Hiroshima and beyond

For the Atomic Bomb Dome, Orizuru Tower and central Hiroshima, walking and the streetcar are often the easiest options. If you're planning a longer trip to rural Yamaguchi or other corners of the Chugoku region, a rental car may be more convenient.

Our stories include practical details where they help, but the best part of travelling is what you find along the way.
