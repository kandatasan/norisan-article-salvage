Primary salvaged first-party experience points:
- Friend owned the 2021-generation Lexus NX for about one year.
- Complaints: price felt high; interior material impression; passenger-side dash felt sparse; meter display felt underwhelming; wireless charging usability; Mark Levinson value-for-price.
- Sale reason was a change in living circumstances, not dissatisfaction with the NX itself.
- Friend's final assessment: fundamentally a good car, no regret about owning it, and would consider buying one again.
